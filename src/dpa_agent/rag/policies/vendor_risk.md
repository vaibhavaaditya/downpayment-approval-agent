# Vendor Risk & Bank Data Policy

## POL-RSK-001 Vendor payment block

Vendors with an active payment block in the vendor master are not eligible for
down payments. The block must be released by the vendor master data team with a
documented reason before any request proceeds.

## POL-RSK-002 Remittance bank verification

The remittance bank on a down-payment request **must match** the bank details in
the vendor master (LFBK). Any mismatch is treated as a potential payment-fraud
indicator: the request is escalated to Financial Control and, if bank details were
changed in the last 30 days, to the fraud desk. Bank changes follow four-eyes
verification outside the approval workflow.

## POL-RSK-003 Delivery performance signal

Vendors with on-time delivery below **80%** over the trailing twelve months carry
elevated risk. A warning is surfaced to the approver; it does not by itself block
a request, but it must be weighed against the size of the advance.

## POL-RSK-004 Watchlist vendors

Vendors on the internal watchlist or in jurisdictions under enhanced due diligence
require escalation regardless of amount. Risk tier is read from the vendor master,
never from request text.
