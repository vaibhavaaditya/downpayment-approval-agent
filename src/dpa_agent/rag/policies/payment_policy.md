# Corporate Payment Policy — Down Payments (FI-AP)

Normative clauses for vendor down-payment (special GL) approval. Clause IDs are
stable citation handles used by the approval agent.

## POL-PAY-001 Down-payment percentage cap

Down payments against a purchase order must not exceed **20% of the PO net value**
per request, or the percentage explicitly agreed in the PO down-payment terms,
whichever is lower. Percentages above the cap require a written exception from the
Head of Procurement, registered before the request is submitted.

## POL-PAY-002 Absolute per-request cap

No single down-payment request may exceed **500,000** in the request currency,
regardless of PO value. Requests above the cap are escalated to the Treasurer.

## POL-PAY-003 Duplicate prevention

A down payment may not be issued for a milestone already financed by a down payment
cleared within the last **90 days** for the same vendor and purchase order.
Suspected duplicates are held and referred to Accounts Payable for reconciliation.

## POL-PAY-004 Cumulative coverage

Prior down payments plus the current request must not exceed the **net value of the
purchase order**. The remaining uncovered PO value must be positive after posting.

## POL-PAY-005 Currency consistency

The request currency must match the purchase order currency. Cross-currency down
payments require treasury conversion approval and are otherwise rejected.
