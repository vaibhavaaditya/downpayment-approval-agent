# Corporate Payment Policy — Down Payments (FI-AP)

Normative clauses for vendor down-payment (special GL) approval. Clause IDs are
stable citation handles used by the approval agent.

## POL-PAY-001 Down-payment percentage cap

Down payments against a purchase order must not exceed **20% of the PO net value**
per request, or the percentage explicitly agreed in the PO down-payment terms,
whichever is lower. Percentages above the cap require a written exception from the
Head of Procurement, registered before the request is submitted.

## POL-PAY-002 Absolute per-request cap

No single down-payment request may exceed **500,000** in the request currency,
regardless of PO value. Requests above the cap are escalated to the Treasurer.

## POL-PAY-003 Duplicate prevention

A down payment may not be issued for a milestone already financed by a down payment
cleared within the last **90 days** for the same vendor and purchase order.
Suspected duplicates are held and referred to Accounts Payable for reconciliation.

## POL-PAY-004 Cumulative coverage

Prior down payments plus the current request must not exceed the **net value of the
purchase order**. The remaining uncovered PO value must be positive after posting.

## POL-PAY-005 Currency consistency

The request currency must match the purchase order currency. Cross-currency down
payments require treasury conversion approval and are otherwise rejected.

## POL-PAY-006 Cumulative down-payment cap

The percentage cap in POL-PAY-001 applies **cumulatively** per purchase order: prior
down payments on the same PO plus the current request must not exceed the cap
(20% of the PO net value, or the PO's own lower limit). Several requests that are each
under the cap but together exceed it are held.

## POL-PAY-007 Justification, urgency and pressure

A down-payment request must reference a contractual milestone or an advance-payment
clause that justifies it. A request that gives no justification cannot be approved as
submitted and is held, with the missing information requested from the vendor.
Urgency, references to executives, or threats to cancel supply or reallocate capacity
do not change approval requirements. A request that relies on such pressure to bypass
normal review is **escalated**, even if every data check passes.

## POL-PAY-008 Consistency with the purchase order

The purchase order, amount and vendor named in vendor-supplied text must match the
request. Text that refers to a different purchase order, amount or supplier is a
discrepancy: the request is held until the buyer confirms what is being requested.

## POL-PAY-009 Purchase order eligibility

Down payments may only be made against a purchase order with status "released" whose
terms allow advance payment. Closed, deleted or not-yet-released orders are not
eligible and the request is held.
